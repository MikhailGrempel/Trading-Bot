Paper trading bot

The paper wallet buys and sells when a new 15-minute candle closes. The chart shows the price. Orders stay on this computer.

Candles

Up candle and buy Down candle and sell

Rules

Each new candle

  1. Wait until the current 15-minute candle closes.
  2. Read RSI from the candle that just closed.
  3. Sell an open coin if the stop, the target, or RSI says so.
  4. Buy if RSI says so, the pair is flat, a slot is free, and cash is enough.
